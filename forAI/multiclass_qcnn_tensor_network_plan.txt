MUC TIEU
Tao mot model 10 lop MNIST ma toan bo bo phan trich xuat dac trung va phan loai nam trong mach QCNN; mo phong mach do bang tensor network (TN) de train tren PyTorch/GPU. KHONG them CNN frontend. Nguon duoc hieu la bai “Multi-Class Quantum Convolutional Neural Networks” cua Mordacci, Ferrari, Amoretti (arXiv:2404.12741) va repository ma tac gia lien ket. Nguon paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.12741
Code tham chieu: https://github.com/qslab-unipr/qcnn

1. CAN PHAN BIET: MODEL HIEN TAI VA MACH TRONG BAI

Model FullQuantumQCNN hien tai trong `full_quantum_32x32/`:
- Anh 28x28 duoc pad thanh 32x32; moi pixel thanh mot qubit/site product-state qua RY encoding, tong 1.024 site.
- QuantumTNCore ghep cac cap site ke nhau bang mot gate conv va mot gate pool, trace bot mot site; lam 7 block de con 8 qubit.
- Sau do co strongly-entangling core 8 qubit; readout Pauli full9 + Linear 10 lop.
- Co 3 nhanh TN doc lap; hien tai ca 3 nhanh mac dinh dung cung row ordering.
- Day la mot mach QCNN/TN tu thiet ke; no KHONG phai mạch trong paper duoc port nguyen ban.

Mach 10-lop trong code tham chieu paper:
- Bat dau voi 8 qubit, khong phai 1.024 qubit/site.
- Nhánh amplitude: PCA anh 28x28 thanh vector 256 chieu, sau do amplitude-encode vao 8 qubit. Nhánh angle trong repo dung 8 thanh phan PCA de angle-encode; de port lan dau nen chon amplitude vi ket qua paper bao cao nhánh nay cao hon trong bang doi encoding.
- Co circuit F1 tien xu ly/pre-convolution, circuit convolution F2 (SU(4), 15 tham so cho mot block) tren cac cap ke nhau theo ca luot chan/le, va pooling circuit hoc duoc.
- Cac pooling chon cap qubit cu the: sau pooling dau con 6 wire hoat dong; sau pooling thu hai con 4; convolution tiep tren 4 wire.
- Cho 10 lop, do paper/code lay probability tren cac wire [0,2,4,6], tuong ung 4 qubit do va 16 outcome co the; class 0..9 duoc anh xa theo thu tu ket qua trong code paper. Khong thay readout Pauli full9 + Linear cua project vao ban port trung thanh.
- Paper co classical preprocessing PCA va classical optimizer Adam; “khong CNN frontend” khong co nghia la khong co buoc preprocessing/optimizer co dien.

2. NHUNG GI CAN PORT VAO TN

Tao implementation rieng, de vi du `full_quantum_qcnn_paper_tn/` hoac module `models/paper_qcnn_tn.py`; khong sua de lam mat model cu/checkpoint cu. Khi port phai giu dung do thi mach va chia se tham so:

A. Data/encoding
1. Doc MNIST grayscale 28x28, flatten 784 pixel va fit PCA(256) CHI tren cac mau train.
2. Dung PCA da fit de transform validation; chi transform test sau khi chot model. Khong fit PCA tren validation/test de tranh leakage.
3. Chuan hoa amplitude vector theo L2 va dat vao 8-qubit amplitude state. Khong thay amplitude encoding bang 1 RY cho tung pixel; hai encoding tao feature map va mach khac nhau.
4. Neu TN backend khong tao amplitude state dung chuan xuat phat, can bieu dien 256 amplitude thanh tensor rank-8 co moi bond vat ly dim=2. Kiem tra chuan hoa ||x||=1 va phan bo dau am truoc khi train.

B. Tensor-network circuit
1. Bieu dien moi gate 1-qubit/2-qubit thanh tensor voi cac chi so vao/ra; noi cac wire thanh bond va contraction theo topo circuit.
2. Port F1 dung dung gate/parameter sharing tu `unitary.py` tham chieu; F1 khong phai CNN va cung khong duoc bo qua.
3. Port F2 la ansatz SU(4) dung trong source (15 parameter angles), ap dung tren cac cap theo thu tu chan va le nhu circuit goc.
4. Port pooling CRZ -> X -> CRX va cach bo/trace wire dung nhu source. Dung dung tap wire sau moi pool; khong thay bang QuantumConvPoolBlock hien tai neu no lam doi topo/ansatz.
5. Sau cac convolution/pooling, doc probability tren wire [0,2,4,6] theo thu tu output cua PennyLane. Map 10 digit theo dung class-index mapping. Dung loss tren 10 class probability theo code tham chieu; khong doi sang Pauli readout/Linear khi dang reproduction.
6. Dung autograd qua tensor contraction cho tham so gate. Neu can contraction xap xi/truncation, ghi ro bond dimension va sai so; ban reproduction dau tien nen contraction chinh xac neu kha thi.

C. Quan he voi code dang co
- `QuantumTNCore` hien tai duoc xay quanh dau vao product states tung site; moi block hoc mot LearnedUnitary2Q conv/pool chung cho cac cap va giam so site mot nua. Sau TN core co StronglyEntanglingCore va Pauli readout.
- Paper circuit co 8-qubit amplitude state PCA, F1 tien xu ly, SU(4) F2 va pooling/active-wire pattern rieng; do do chi thay input size/FQ_ARCH khong the tao thanh paper architecture.
- Can viet core/mach paper rieng de bieu dien state amplitude co the entangle va cac wire pattern chinh xac. Co the dung lai ham luu checkpoint, history, plotting, split va training-loop neu khong lam thay doi circuit.
- Ten output/checkpoint rieng, vi state_dict va tham so khong tuong thich checkpoint `full_quantum`/`full_quantum_32x32` cu.

3. TRAINING VA SO SANH CONG BANG

Thiet lap reproducing-first:
- 10 lop, dung toan bo tap train cua MNIST; de duy tri protocol project, tach 50.000 train / 10.000 validation voi split seed 20260911. Giữ MNIST official test rieng den khi chot model.
- Adam, lr=0.01, batch=64, seed=43 la setting duoc README cua repo paper ghi; paper/code co sai khac ve epoch: README dat default 2 epoch, trong khi bang ket qua mo ta 10 epoch. Ghi ro so epoch va luu config; khong tu dong xem hai setting la mot. Chay reproduction theo code truoc, sau do moi tune so epoch/LR o run moi.
- Bài paper dung MNIST 4/6/8/10 class va doi chieu voi CNN rat nho, parameter-matched. Khi muc tieu la 99% tren project, chay ca all-10-class tren split validation hien tai va giu CNN thuần 99% lam benchmark thuc te; khong dua con so CNN nho trong paper thanh baseline cua project.
- Theo paper/source, 10-class QCNN co ket qua bao cao 57% va CNN doi chieu 38% trong setting so sanh duoc mo ta; day la outperformance tuong doi, KHONG phai 99%. Vi vay paper cung cap ansatz de port/chay thu, khong dam bao dat muc tieu 99%.
- Chon checkpoint bang validation loss/accuracy, ghi confusion matrix va accuracy tung digit, dac biet cac cap nham 4<->9, 3<->5, 9<->7. Khong dung validation de update model; khong xem test trong qua trinh tune.

4. CAC BUOC LAM DE XAC NHAN PORT DUNG

1. Giu source code goc paper ben canh implementation TN moi de doi chieu gate order, wire order, parameter count, encoding va output index.
2. Truoc khi train MNIST dai, chon vai input va mot bo tham so gate co dinh: so sanh probability/logit cua PennyLane circuit voi TN contraction. Sai lech phai nam trong tolerance so hoc da dat truoc.
3. So sanh gradient cua mot loss nho theo tung tham so gate giua PennyLane va PyTorch autograd; neu output trung nhung gradient sai thi training TN se khong reproduce paper.
4. Kiem tra mot batch co output shape 10, probability hop le, loss huu han, va optimizer lam loss co the giam tren mot batch nho.
5. Sau cac kiem tra tren moi chay full 50k/10k. Luu config, best/last checkpoint, history va phan tich loi trong thu muc run rieng.
