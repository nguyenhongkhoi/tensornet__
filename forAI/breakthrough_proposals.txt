2. CHAN DOAN 3 NUT THAT CAU TRUC COT LOI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ngoai cac yeu to ngoai vi nhu Data Augmentation va MLP Readout, mo hinh FullQuantumQCNN
hien tai dang bi gioi han boi 3 nut that cau truc nam ngay trong Tensor Network:

NUT THAT A: MAT TUONG QUAN (ENTANGLEMENT) DO PARTIAL TRACE SAU MOI TANG POOLING
- Co che hien tai trong QuantumConvPoolBlock:
  1) Moi block nhan 2k site, ghep thanh k cap site lien ke: (0,1), (2,3),...
  2) Ap dung LearnedUnitary2Q conv (4x4), tiep theo LearnedUnitary2Q pool (4x4) len tung cap.
  3) Ham _trace_second_qubit thuc hien partial trace bo hoan toan qubit thu 2 cua moi cap.
  4) Dau ra moi block la mot danh sach cac ma tran mat do 2x2 doc lap (factorized product state).
- He qua: Toan bo tuong quan (quantum entanglement / correlation) giua cac cap khac nhau
  bi xoa sach hoan toan sau moi block.
- Qua 5 block pooling o ban 16x16, mo hinh bi "mat tri nho toan cuc" 5 lan lien tiep.
  Chi den khi con lai 8 qubit cuoi cung o merge stage thi strongly-entangling moi duoc
  ap dung de entangle lai. Day la diem nghen bieu dien nghiem trong nhat cua mo hinh.

NUT THAT B: FLATTEN 2D THANH 1D VA CA 3 NHANH CUNG NHIN ROW-MAJOR
- Anh 16x16 duoc trai phang theo thu tu hang (row-major) thanh 256 site.
- Block 1 ghep (pixel_0, pixel_1), (pixel_2, pixel_3)... giup cac pixel canh nhau theo
  hang ngang duoc tuong tac som.
- Tuy nhien, cac pixel canh nhau theo chieu doc (cach nhau 16 vi tri) bi tach roi
  hoan toan va chi co the gap nhau o cac tang pooling rat sau (tang 4, tang 5).
- Trong baseline, ca 3 nhanh quantum deu de mac dinh spatial_orders=None (tuc ca 3 deu
  nhan thu tu row). Ba nhanh vi vay mac cung mot blind spot khong gian, de hoc giai
  phap trung lap thay vi bo tro cho nhau.

NUT THAT C: CO CHE RE-UPLOADING QUA THO SO
- Trong reupload.py, sau khi TN core gom 256 site con 8 qubit, co che re-upload chi tinh
  trung binh cong goc RY cua cac pixel thuoc nhom anh xa toi qubit do:
    angles = angles.reshape(angles.shape[0], -1, self.final_qubits).mean(dim=1)
    angles = angles * self.reupload_scale[None, :]
- Moi qubit chi nhan duy nhat 1 goc quay trung binh voi 1 scalar scale (tong cong chi
  +24 tham so cho ca 3 nhanh). Thong tin chi tiet ve net ve va do tuong phan cuc bo
  bi san phang khi lay trung binh, lam giam tac dung phuc hoi thong tin cua re-uploading.

3. NAM DE XUAT CAI TIEN MANG TINH DOT PHA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

DE XUAT 1: INTERMEDIATE ENTANGLING LAYER (UU TIEN HANG DAU, THUAN QUANTUM)
- Muc tieu: Khac phuc Nut that A ma khong lam pha vo tinh chat quantum cua Tensor Network.
- Thiet ke: Chen mot lop entangling nhe giua cac tang pooling (vi du sau block 2 va block 4).
  + Sau block 2, con 64 qubit (hoac sau block 3 con 32 qubit).
  + Thay vi de cac qubit doc lap di tiep vao block sau, ap dung 1 luot brickwall entangling
    (2-qubit unitary) tren cac cap so le (1,2), (3,4)... MA KHONG TRACE BO QUBIT NAO.
  + Buoc nay cho phep thong tin lan truyen cheo giua cac nhanh cua cay tensor truoc khi bi pool tiep.
- Chi phi: Chi them khoang 32 - 64 tham so unitary, chi phi tinh toan tang khong dang ke.

DE XUAT 2: SPATIAL DIVERSITY (ROW / COLUMN / MORTON) CHO 3 NHANH (CHI PHI 0 THAM SO)
- Muc tieu: Khac phuc Nut that B ngay lap tuc bang code da co san trong full_quantum_qcnn.py.
- Thiet ke: Cau hinh FQ_ARCH voi:
    spatial_orders=["row", "column", "morton"]
  + Nhanh 1 (row): Gom cac pixel theo hang ngang, bat cac dac trung nam ngang.
  + Nhanh 2 (column): Gom cac pixel theo cot doc, bat cac net dung va do cao net chu.
  + Nhanh 3 (morton / Z-curve): Gom cac patch 2x2, 4x4 cuc bo 2D, bao toan tinh lien thong khong gian.
- Chi phi: 0 tham so them, khong tang thoi gian tinh toan, giai quyet su trung lap cua 3 nhanh.

DE XUAT 3: MULTI-SCALE RE-UPLOADING (PHUC HOI THONG TIN PIXEL GOC)
- Muc tieu: Khac phuc Nut that C, dua thong tin hinh hoc pixel vao sau pooling mot cach chi tiet hon.
- Thiet ke: Thay vi 1 scalar trung binh cho moi qubit:
  + Dung mot module chieu nhe (vi du Linear(32 -> 16) -> GELU -> Linear(16 -> 4)) de trich xuat
    4 goc quay dai dien tu 32 pixel dau vao ung voi moi qubit.
  + Thuc hien 4 vong quay re-upload lien tiep ket hop entangling gate truoc khi vao merge stage.
- Chi phi: Khoang 3.000 - 5.000 tham so, thoi gian tinh toan tang rat it.

DE XUAT 4: RESIDUAL CLASSICAL BOND MEMORY (TICH HOP DUONG TRUYEN RESIDUAL)
- Muc tieu: Bu dap hoan toan luong thong tin bi mat mat qua partial trace.
- Thiet ke: Xay dung mot nhanh phu song song nhe (1D Conv stride=2 qua 5 block) rut gon
  256 gia tri pixel xuong 8 gia tri dac trung co dien.
  + 8 gia tri nay duoc ghep truc tiep vao vector 276 Pauli features truoc khi dua vao Readout Head.
- Danh gia: Ranh gioi giua hybrid va full quantum bi mo di doi chut, nhung day la co che
  chong mat thong tin cuc ky hieu qua trong thi giac may tinh.

DE XUAT 5: LEARNED POSITIONAL ANGLE ENCODING (MA HOA CO TRONG SO KHONG GIAN)
- Muc tieu: Thay the co che ma hoa RY co dinh (theta = pi * p).
- Thiet ke:
    theta_i = p_i * w_i + b_i  (voi w_i, b_i la tham so hoc rieng cho tung site i = 0..255)
- Y nghia: Vung trung tam anh (noi net chu tap trung) se hoc duoc do nhay ma hoa goc
  khac biet hoan toan so voi vung ria ngoai (noi chu yeu la vien den).
- Chi phi: Them dung 512 tham so (256 scale + 256 bias).
