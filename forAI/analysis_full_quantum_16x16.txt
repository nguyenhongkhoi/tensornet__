1. BASELINE 16x16 DANG CHAY NHU THE NAO

Du lieu va danh gia
- MNIST official train duoc chia co dinh thanh 50.000 train va 10.000 validation,
  split seed 20260911. Official test khong duoc dung trong run nay.
- Anh duoc ToTensor ve [0,1], chuyen grayscale neu can, sau do resize bilinear
  tu 28x28 xuong 16x16 va flatten thanh 256 pixel sites.
- Moi pixel p duoc ma hoa thanh qubit RY voi theta = pi*p:
  cos(theta/2)|0> + sin(theta/2)|1>.
- Khong co data augmentation trong baseline.

TN core va readout
- Moi nhanh nhan 256 qubit/site dau vao va giam con 8 site qua
  log2(256/8)=5 QuantumConvPoolBlock.
- Mot block ghep tung cap site (0,1), (2,3),...; ap dung LearnedUnitary2Q conv,
  roi LearnedUnitary2Q pool, sau do trace bo qubit thu hai trong tung cap. Trong
  mot block, cung mot cap tham so conv/pool duoc dung lap lai cho moi cap site.
- Dau ra block la danh sach cac ma tran mat do 2x2 rieng cho tung site con lai.
  Block ke tiep ghep cac site nay nhu cac trang thai doc lap.
- Sau 5 block, 8 qubit duoc ghep thanh joint density matrix 256x256. Moi nhanh
  chay 4 layer strongly-entangling tren 8 qubit roi readout Pauli + lop Linear.
- Readout full9 co 24 dac trung Pauli don va 252 tuong quan Pauli theo cap,
  tong 276 feature cho lop Linear 10 lop.
- quantum_channels=3 tao ba nhanh tn_core/readout rieng, doc cung anh va trung
  binh logits. FQ_ARCH khong truyen spatial_orders; model mac dinh row cho ca ba
  nhanh. Nhu vay ba nhanh co trong so rieng nhung cung mot cach sap xep pixel.
- Phase 3 them 8 reupload_scale cho moi nhanh, tong +24 tham so; moi scale bat
  dau bang 0 de warm-start giu nguyen hanh vi model phase 2.
- Tong so tham so ghi trong code la 13.110 truoc reupload va 13.134 sau reupload.
  Rieng ba lop Linear full9 co 3 x (276*10 + 10) = 8.310 tham so.

Lich train
- Phase 1: tu dau, Adam lr=0.002, 20 epoch, batch 64; chon checkpoint theo
  validation accuracy.
- Phase 2: tiep tuc tu best phase 1, Adam moi lr=0.0002, 5 epoch; van chon theo
  validation accuracy.
- Phase 3: them reupload va warm-start tu phase 2; Adam core lr=0.0001,
  readout lr=0.001; ReduceLROnPlateau factor 0.5/patience 3; toi da 60 epoch,
  early-stop patience 10; chon checkpoint theo validation loss.
- Tong log co 85 epoch: 20 + 5 + 60. Sau khi scheduler xu ly validation loss
  cua global epoch 75, LR phase 3 giam xuong 0.00005/0.0005; dong log epoch 76
  la dong dau tien ghi LR moi.

Ket qua log
- Phase 1 dat validation accuracy cao nhat 96.82% (global epoch 19).
- Phase 2 dat validation accuracy cao nhat 97.25% (global epoch 22).
- Phase 3 dat validation accuracy cao nhat 97.73% (global epoch 66).
- Checkpoint duoc chon theo validation loss o epoch 82: val loss 0.074029,
  val accuracy 97.71%. Vi criterion chon checkpoint la val loss, ket qua summary
  97.71%, du co epoch khac dat accuracy toi da 97.73%.
- Epoch 85: train accuracy 98.582%, val accuracy 97.63%, train loss 0.04925,
  val loss 0.07451. Khoang cach train-validation accuracy khoang 0.95 diem phan
  tram: co generalization gap nho-vua, chua cho thay overfit nang.
- 97.71% tren 10.000 validation mau tuong ung khoang 229 mau sai. De len 99%
  tren cung validation set can con khoang 100 mau sai, tuc can giam them xap xi
  129 loi so voi checkpoint 97.71%.
- Phase 3 best accuracy va best loss khong cung epoch. Khi so sanh cac run, luu
  ca hai metric va ghi ro chon checkpoint theo tieu chi nao.
- Ba dong epoch_seconds dau tien trong history la 185.188k, 128.308k,
  114.704k giay, khac han cac dong con lai. Khong nen dung ba dong nay de uoc
  tinh runtime. Median cac dong con lai xap xi 1.400 giay/epoch phase 1,
  1.408 giay/epoch phase 2 va 1.004 giay/epoch phase 3. Tong thoi gian train
  thuc te theo cac median nay vao khoang 26.5 gio.

2. CAC YEU TO CO THE GIOI HAN HIEU SUAT

A. Mat tuong quan giua cac site sau pooling — uu tien nghien cuu cao nhat
Trong QuantumConvPoolBlock, sau khi moi cap duoc ap dung conv/pool, code chi giu
reduced density matrix cua qubit song sot va trace qubit con lai. Tuong quan giua
cap nay voi cac cap khac khong duoc mang sang block ke tiep. Vi vay 5 block dau
khong duy tri mot trang thai toan cuc/MPS co bond noi cac pixel; no truyen cac
trang thai don-qubit da factorize. Chi 8 site cuoi moi duoc ghep va entangle lai.

Day la bottleneck bieu dien co the quan trong: dac trung net nam trong quan he
khong gian cua nhieu pixel co the bi mat truoc khi den merge stage. Tuy nhien,
day la tinh chat kien truc da chon, khong tu dong la loi code.

Luu y ve bond dimension: code 16x16 hien tai khong co tham so bond_dimension de
tang. Moi site truyen ma tran 2x2; giai doan cuoi tao density matrix 256x256 cho
8 qubit. Muon bond dimension chi ro thi phai viet/doi TN core sang MPS/TT hoac
TN co bond noi cac site. Tang final_qubits khong tuong duong tang MPS bond va
co the lam joint density matrix tang rat nhanh.

B. Sap xep 2D thanh chuoi 1D
Pixel duoc flatten row-major va cac block luon ghep cap site ke nhau. Cach nay
giu cac cap pixel ngang gan nhau o block dau, nhung topo cac block sau la mot
cay tren chuoi, khong phai convolution/pooling 2D chuan. Ma nguon da co ho tro
row, column, Morton permutation; baseline mac dinh dung row cho ca ba nhanh.
Morton co the dua cac patch 2D gan nhau vao cung nhanh cua cay som hon.

C. Ba nhanh hien khong co du lieu vao khac nhau
Ba nhanh co cac tham so hoc rieng nen co the thanh experts khac nhau, nhung ca
ba nhan cung mot row-major input va cung loss chung. Khong co loss phu hay
gating buoc chung chuyen mon. Chung co the bo sung nhau, nhung cung co the hoc
giai phap gan giong nhau; compute gan ba lan mot nhanh. Day la dieu can do bang
ablation, khong nen mac dinh ba nhanh tot hon mot nhanh.

D. Giam 28x28 xuong 16x16
Bilinear resize loai bo hon nua chieu moi anh truoc encoding. Net nho, do day
net va chieu cong cua cac chu so co the bi lam tron. Loi tren cac cap 4->9,
9->4, 3->5, 9->7, 7->9 trong train CSV da gui co the mot phan do net ve chu,
nhung can xem confusion matrix validation de xac nhan.

Khong nen ket luan rang 32x32 se tu dong tot hon: code 32x32 pad zero len
1024 sites, so block tang tu 5 len 7 va so site-pair contraction tang xap xi
4 lan moi nhanh. Log 32x32 nguoi dung gui co epoch phase 1 mat khoang 4.2k-4.9k
giay va val cao nhat thay trong cac epoch do la 96.72% o epoch 14. Do run do
chua hoan thanh phase 2/3, khong the so ket qua phase 1 nay voi checkpoint cuoi
97.71% cua model 16x16.

E. Readout chiem phan lon so tham so
Ba Linear full9 chua 8.310/13.134 tham so sau reupload, xap xi 63%. Day la
readout co classical linear layer, du phan trich xuat la quantum-inspired/TN.
So tham so nhieu khong chung minh overfit; gap train-validation chi khoang 1
diem %. Nhung full9 va readout ZZ nen duoc so sanh de biet them tuong quan
Pauli co thuc su giup validation hay chi tang do phuc tap.

F. Khoi tao gate gan identity va parameterization unitary
LearnedUnitary khoi tao raw matrices voi scale 0.01 va dung matrix exponential;
gate ban dau gan identity. Moi unitary duoc tao tu ma tran Hermitian/skew de
giu tinh unitary, nhung cac phan tu raw co mot so bac tu do trung lap. Day la
gia thuyet ve toc do/hinh dang toi uu, khong co dau hieu trong log rang day la
nguyen nhan chinh. Khong nen doi init_scale cung luc voi thay doi khac.

G. Augmentation va regularization chua duoc thu trong baseline
Baseline chi dung pixel [0,1]. Augmentation dich nhe/rotate nhe co the lam
model chiu duoc sai lech net chu, nhung xoay/dich manh co the thay doi nhan hoac
lam mat chi tiet. Gap train-val vua phai nen regularization co the thu, nhung
khong co dau hieu overfit nang de uu tien weight decay truoc bottleneck TN.

H. Mot seed va chon checkpoint
Config ghi seed=0 cho mot lan chay. Ket qua 97.71/97.73 chua biet do on dinh
qua cac lan khoi tao. Validation duoc dung trong qua trinh chon epoch; khong
nen tiep tuc chinh tham so tren cung validation ma khong co test set cuoi cung.

3. CAC THU NGHIEM KHA THI, XEP THEO UU TIEN

Buoc 0 — dong bang baseline truoc khi sua code
- Giu checkpoint va config 16x16 goc; luu ket qua tren validation hien tai.
- Neu checkpoint nay chua tung duoc danh gia tren official MNIST test, chay
  test mot lan sau khi da chot model. Khong dung test de chinh tham so.
- Tao confusion matrix tren validation theo lop va cac cap loi; khong suy ra
  loi validation tu 701 loi trong train CSV.

Thu 1 — thu 1 nhanh voi cung du lieu va split
- Doi quantum_channels=3 thanh 1, giu 16x16, FQ core, readout, optimizer, seed,
  split va lich train nhu baseline. Luu run vao thu muc rieng.
- Muc dich: xem ba nhanh co dang bo sung nhau hay chi tang compute. Mot nhanh
  co the giam thoi gian va so tham so, nhung co the giam accuracy; day la
  ablation, khong phai khang dinh model nho se tot hon.

Thu 2 — tao da dang khong gian cho ba nhanh
- So sanh rieng baseline [row,row,row] voi [row,column,morton]. Code model da
  ho tro spatial_orders, nhung can truyen cau hinh nay trong run moi.
- Giu cac tham so khac nhu nhau. Morton duoc uu tien thu vi no nhom patch 2D;
  ket qua validation moi quyet dinh co giu hay khong.

Thu 3 — augmentation nhe tren day du 50k train
- Warm-start tu checkpoint tot nhat 16x16, fine-tune 5-10 epoch tren toan bo
  50k train, validation van bat bien va khong cap nhat trong validation.
- Thu dich toi da 1-2 pixel va goc xoay nho (vi du ±5 do), ap dung tren anh
  28x28 truoc resize 16x16; bat dau muc nhe.
- Dung learning rate nho, vi du core 1e-5/readout 1e-4, early stop theo
  validation loss va luu checkpoint moi trong thu muc moi. Day la diem khoi
  dau de thu, khong phai LR da duoc chung minh toi uu.
- Cac lan fine-tune chi tren 701 mau loi da cho train accuracy hard subset
  tang nhung validation quanh 97.7% va val loss xau dan trong log nguoi dung gui.
  Khong lap lai hard-only; neu can replay thi giu du lieu train binh thuong.

Thu 4 — so sanh readout full9 voi ZZ
- Giữ TN core, seed, du lieu va schedule; chi doi pair_correlators full9 sang zz.
- Day la run khac architecture readout, nen khong the nap truc tiep trong so
  Linear full9 sang lop ZZ co kich thuoc khac. Can train rieng va luu rieng.
- So ca validation accuracy, validation loss, confusion matrix va runtime.

Thu 5 — TN co bond noi thuc su
- Neu muc tieu la giai quyet bottleneck thong tin, thiet ke TN/MPS co bond
  chi=2 truoc, sau do chi=4 neu tai nguyen cho phep. Chi la mot thay doi kien
  truc moi; khong coi chi la flag tren model hien tai.
- Bao cao memory, runtime, va validation accuracy. Tang chi lam chi phi tang va
  khong bao dam accuracy tang.

Thu 6 — toi uu khoi tao/optimizer sau cac ablation
- Chi sau khi biet branch/readout/spatial ordering nao co ich, thu rieng
  init_scale 0.03 so voi 0.01 hoac AdamW voi weight_decay nho tren run moi.
- Hien tai Adam va ReduceLROnPlateau da dua val loss xuong 0.0740; doi optimizer
  khong phai huong dau tien co kha nang giai quyet mat tuong quan/TN bottleneck.
