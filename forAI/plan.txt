1. Trang thai va checkpoint goc

  + runs/full_quantum_sample_analysis/train_per_sample.csv (50.000 mau train)
  + runs/full_quantum_sample_analysis/validation_per_sample.csv (10.000 mau validation)
- Neu chua chay xong tren may train, hai CSV chua duoc tao day du. Cho den khi terminal in dong train/validation hoan tat moi copy CSV sang may lam viec nay.
- Moi dong CSV co split, dataset_index, true_label, predicted_label, correct, confidence, true_class_probability, margin va cross_entropy. dataset_index la vi tri mau trong MNIST train goc, dung de tim lai dung anh.
- Script uu tien checkpoint runs/full_quantum/fq_phase3_reupload_best.pt; neu khong co ban da giai nen thi no tim trong runs/full_quantum_v2, thu muc full_quantum hoac file ZIP o thu muc du an.
- Checkpoint goc nen dung de warm-start la fq_phase3_reupload_best.pt (best theo validation loss), khong ghi de len file goc. Trong log, checkpoint nay dat val loss 0.0740291 va val accuracy 97.71% tai epoch 82. Train accuracy cuoi log la 98.582% tai epoch 85; day la metric tong hop, khong phai danh sach tung anh.

2. Doc CSV va lap tap mau kho
Sau khi co CSV:
1) Mo train_per_sample.csv bang Excel/LibreOffice.
2) Loc correct = 0 de lay cac mau train du doan sai. Sap xep true_class_probability tang dan hoac cross_entropy giam dan de xem mau mo hinh that su kho.
3) Co the danh dau them mau dung nhung khong chac (confidence thap) va mau bien gioi (margin thap). Chon nguong sau khi xem phan phoi thuc te; khong tu dong xem moi mau confidence vua phai la mau kem.
4) Dung dataset_index de luu danh sach mau kho, vi du hard_train_indices.csv. Bao cao so luong theo true_label va cap (true_label, predicted_label).
5) Dung validation_per_sample.csv de danh gia ket qua theo cung tieu chi, tao danh sach hard-validation co dinh de so sanh truoc/sau. Tuyet doi khong dua cac index validation vao tap train/fine-tune.

3. Chuan bi fine-tune
1) Dung cung MNIST official train, ToTensor, split-seed 20260911 va dung source code/architecture da tao checkpoint.
2) Giu nguyen checkpoint goc; tao hash SHA256 cua checkpoint va config. Luu moi lan fine-tune trong output directory moi.
3) Checkpoint phai duoc giai nen ra file neu se nap bang --fq-warm-start cua run_convergence.py; tuy chon khac la bo sung cho training script doc checkpoint truc tiep trong ZIP.
4) Training pipeline can nhan danh sach dataset_index tu CSV. --train-subset hien tai chi chon N mau dau cua train split, khong loc theo danh sach ID CSV, nen chua the su dung truc tiep cho hard-example training.
5) Truoc khi train, pipeline phai doi chieu cac index voi train split duoc tao lai bang seed 20260911 va tu choi neu co index thuoc validation.

4. Cac thu nghiem fine-tune de xuat
Muc tieu la tap trung sua loi kho, nhung van giu kha nang tren cac mau con lai.
- Baseline goc: chay inference/metric tren validation va hard-validation truoc fine-tune de co moc so sanh.
- Thu nghiem A (uu tien): fine-tune tren toan bo train split, dung weighted sampler de mau sai/kho co tan suat lay mau cao hon. Chay hai muc trong so mau kho 2x va 3x.
- Thu nghiem B: fine-tune tren tap mau kho thuoc train split ket hop 25-50% mau dung lay ngau nhien tu train split. Tranh hard-only de giam overfit va quen cac mau de.
- Warm-start tu runs/full_quantum/fq_phase3_reupload_best.pt. Neu checkpoint nam trong ZIP, giai nen rieng checkpoint nay truoc khi truyen duong dan vao run_convergence.py; khong can train lai tu dau.
- Khoi dau voi Adam, batch size 64 neu VRAM cho phep; lr core 1e-5 den 3e-5, lr readout 1e-4 den 3e-4; 5-15 epoch, early stopping 3-5 epoch theo validation loss. Giam LR neu validation loss khong con giam.
- Tao output rieng nhu runs/full_quantum_hard_ft_v1 va ..._v2. Khong bat resume tu output cu va khong ghi de checkpoint goc.
- Muc tieu tham khao cho moi run: loi train duoc uu tien giam, nhung khong de validation loss/accuracy tong the xau ro rang.

5. Danh gia va quyet dinh giu checkpoint
- Chay lai inference tung mau tren cung 10.000 validation sau fine-tune.
- So sanh checkpoint goc va fine-tuned: validation accuracy, cross-entropy/loss, macro-F1, confusion matrix, per-class recall, so mau sai va so mau sai tren hard-validation.
- Lap danh sach mau duoc sua va mau moi bi sai; kiem tra theo nhan that va cap nham lan.
- Chi chon checkpoint theo validation. Khong dung MNIST test de chinh tham so; neu can bao cao ket qua cuoi, danh gia test mot lan sau khi chot.
- Chi giu checkpoint fine-tuned neu loi tren hard-validation giam ma hieu nang validation tong the khong suy giam dang ke. Luu config, seed, sampler/weights, checkpoint va hash cung bao cao.

8. Ky vong loi ich va rui ro khi fine-tune mau kho
- Fine-tune co the giam loi tren cac mau train kho va co the cai thien hard-validation neu cac mau nay the hien quy luat chung ma model chua hoc du. Muc cai thien khong the bao dam truoc; can xac nhan tren validation chua tung dua vao gradient.
- Neu chi train lap lai mot tap hard nho, model co the hoc thuoc cac anh do (overfit), tang do tu tin khong dung, hoac quen cac mau train de (catastrophic forgetting). Khi do hard subset co the tot len nhung validation tong the, cac lop khac, hoac cac mau goc lai kem di.
- Cac mau sai co the la anh mơ ho, gan nhan khong chuan, hoac nhan that su kho phan biet. Kiem tra mot so mau va cap (nhan that, nhan du doan) truoc khi tang trong so; khong sua nhan chi de model du doan theo mong muon.
- Thiet ke giam rui ro: warm-start checkpoint best; giu nguyen validation; uu tien weighted sampling tren toan bo train voi hard examples duoc lay thuong hon (thu 2x, 3x), hoac hard train ket hop mau train binh thuong; khong train hard-only trong run dau.
- Dung learning rate thap, it epoch, early stopping theo validation loss; luu checkpoint moi o thu muc rieng. Neu validation loss xau di lien tuc hoac accuracy tong the sut ro, dung run va quay lai checkpoint goc.
- So sanh it nhat: (1) checkpoint goc, (2) fine-tune toan train voi hard weight 2x, (3) fine-tune voi hard weight 3x hoac hard + mau binh thuong. Dung cung validation split va cung cach tinh metric cho tat ca.
- Chi chap nhan model moi neu loi/ty le sai tren hard-validation giam va validation tong the khong suy giam dang ke; theo doi them per-class recall, confusion matrix, macro-F1, cross-entropy va cac mau truoc day dung nhung sau fine-tune bi sai.
- Neu hard-validation tot hon nhung validation tong the giam, day la trade-off chua chac co loi: khong thay checkpoint goc; ghi ro thay doi theo lop va xac dinh uu tien ung dung truoc khi chon.
- Khong dung official MNIST test de chon sampler, learning rate hay checkpoint. Chi danh gia test mot lan sau khi da chot cau hinh cuoi neu can ket qua bao cao.

12. Yeu cau moi nhat: model moi tinh, train tu phase 1 chi voi 701 mau hard
Yeu cau nay khac ca hai thu nghiem truoc. run_hard_example_finetune.py la fine-tune tu checkpoint da train; replay_v1 la fine-tune tu checkpoint da train co them mau dung. Script rieng da tao: run_hard_only_from_scratch.py.
- Phase 1 pretrain: model khong reupload, Adam lr=2e-3, mac dinh 20 epoch; giu checkpoint phase co validation accuracy cao nhat.
- Phase 2 refine: tiep tuc tu best phase 1, tao Adam moi lr=2e-4, mac dinh 5 epoch; giu checkpoint phase co validation accuracy cao nhat.
- Phase 3 reupload: them reupload parameters, warm-start tu best phase 2 (day la noi bo trong cung run, khong phai checkpoint cu), Adam core lr=1e-4/readout lr=1e-3, ReduceLROnPlateau, toi da 60 epoch va early stopping patience 10 theo validation loss.
- Tat ca ba phase chi cap nhat trong so tren cung 701 mau hard; validation 10.000 mau chi danh gia/chon checkpoint va khong duoc train. MNIST test khong su dung.
- Output rieng runs/full_quantum_hard_from_scratch_v1/: phase1_pretrain_best/last.pt, phase2_refine_best/last.pt, phase3_reupload_best/last.pt, history_full_quantum.csv, from_scratch_config.json, convergence_all.png, convergence_per_model.png va summary.json.
- So sanh ket qua phase3 tren validation voi baseline. Vi 701 mau duoc chon chinh vi model cu sai tren chung va khong dai dien day du 10 lop, khong ky vong mac dinh dat 99%; nguy co overfit cao. 14. Thu nghiem 16x16 FullQuantumQCNN: giu 3 phase, them MLP readout va augmentation
Trang thai quyet dinh: chay mot run moi tu random initialization voi day du 3 phase giong recipe FullQuantum 16x16 goc. Chi thay hai yeu to trong thiet ke: readout Pauli linear thanh MLP, va them RandomAffine nhe vao anh TRAIN. Khong thay augmentation bang Fourier feature mapping trong run nay.

14.1. Vi sao khong dung Fourier feature de thay augmentation
- Data augmentation bien doi truc tiep anh train (o day la xoay/dich nhe), giup mo hinh thay cac bien the hinh hoc co cung nhan. Validation khong augmentation. Xoay chi nen nhe vi cac chu so nhu 6/9 co the bi nham neu bien doi qua lon.
- Fourier feature mapping kinh dien ma hoa toa do (x,y,...) bang cac dac trung sin/cos de MLP hoc ham theo toa do va chi tiet tan so cao; no khong tu sinh anh xoay va khong tu tao tinh bat bien voi goc xoay. Tham chieu: Tancik et al., “Fourier Features Let Networks Learn High Frequency Functions in Low Dimensional Domains”, https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.10739.
- Bài toan hien tai la phan loai MNIST tu tensor pixel 16x16, khong phai implicit coordinate regression/NeRF. Them Fourier positional features se can thiet ke lai cach ma hoa moi site/pixel hoac dau vao model; day la mot bien doi kien truc va co the lam tang kich thuoc/tinh toan, khong phai mot thay the truc tiep cho RandomAffine.
- Neu muon nghien cuu Fourier sau nay, tao mot experiment folder/run rieng, giu cung split, seed va recipe 3 phase; so sanh voi baseline va run augmentation nay. Khong chen Fourier vao run hien tai de tranh khong biet thay doi nao tao khac biet.
- Khong co bao dam augmentation hay MLP se tang accuracy. Muc tieu la do tren cung 10k validation; khong dung test de chinh tham so.

14.2. Cac chi tiet giu nguyen so voi recipe goc
- Model FullQuantumQCNN 16x16, grayscale, 3 quantum channels, 8 final qubits, strongly-entangling core 4 layers, merge_rounds=2, full9 Pauli features; reupload chi duoc them o phase 3.
- MNIST official train split: 50.000 train va 10.000 validation, split seed 20260911; validation khong cap nhat gradient, test set khong su dung.
- Phase 1: khoi tao model tu trong so random; Adam 2e-3, batch 64, 20 epoch; chon state co validation accuracy tot nhat.
- Phase 2: tiep tuc state tot nhat phase 1, khoi tao Adam moi lr=2e-4, batch 64, 5 epoch; tiep tuc chon theo validation accuracy.
- Phase 3: them reupload va tiep tuc tu state tot nhat phase 2; Adam core lr=1e-4, readout lr=1e-3; ReduceLROnPlateau factor=0.5, patience=3, min_lr=1e-7; toi da 60 epoch, early stop sau 10 epoch khong cai thien validation loss; chon checkpoint phase 3 theo validation loss.
- Seed khoi tao mac dinh 0, giong lenh baseline trong forAI/validcheck.txt; augmentation random nen run moi se khong co cung tung minibatch pixel nhu run khong augmentation.
- Toan bo 3 phase dung cung train dataset co RandomAffine(degrees=8, translate=0.05, fill=0) + ToTensor. Validation chi ToTensor. Vi augmentation duoc ap dung ca phase 1-3, day la mot yeu to thay doi duoc kiem soat ro.

14.3. Hai thay doi duoc thu nghiem
- Readout moi cho moi nhanh: Linear(276,256) -> LayerNorm(256) -> GELU -> Dropout(0.1) -> Linear(256,10). Tensor-network core va cach ma hoa anh van duoc giu.
- Augmentation train: RandomAffine degrees=8 va translate=0.05; validation khong bien doi.
- MLP lam tang dang ke so tham so readout va tang nang luc phan loai classical o head. Vi vay neu accuracy tang, ket qua khong the quy het rieng cho augmentation hay rieng cho MLP; muon tach nguyen nhan can chay them ablation (MLP only, augmentation only) tren cung protocol.
