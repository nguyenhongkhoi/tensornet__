1. TONG QUAN DU AN VA MUC TIEU
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Bai toan: Nhan dang chu so viet tay MNIST (10 lop, tu 0 den 9).
- Muc tieu chinh: Nghien cuu, huan luyen va so sanh kha nang hoi tu cua 3 kien truc:
  + Classical CNN: CNN truyen thong (Conv2d -> BatchNorm -> ReLU -> MaxPool -> Linear).
  + Hybrid QCNN: Trich xuat dac trung co dien ket hop lop mach luong tu (Quantum circuit).
  + Full Quantum QCNN: Mang no-ron luong tu / Tensor Network hoan toan xu ly tu anh vao.
- Trong tam cao nhat hien tai la toi uu hoa va danh gia chuyen sau kien truc Full Quantum QCNN,
  dac biet la hanh vi tren cac mau hoc kho (hard examples) va cac mau du doan sai (misclassified).

    pha huan luyen hoac fine-tune nao.

3. KIEN TRUC FULL QUANTUM QCNN VA CHECKPOINT BASELINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Kien truc chi tiet (FQ_ARCH):
  + Kich thuoc anh dau vao: target_size = (16, 16), grayscale = True.
  + So kenh luong tu: quantum_channels = 3.
  + So qubit cuoi cung: final_qubits = 8.
  + Loai entangling: strongly_entangling voi n_layers = 4, merge_rounds = 2.
  + Readout: pauli_observable voi pair_correlators = "full9", init_scale = 0.01.
  + Co che Re-uploading: Bo sung them 24 tham so reupload_scale (tong cong ~13.134 tham so).
- Quy trinh huan luyen goc 3 giai doan (3-Phase Recipe):
  + Phase 1 (Pretrain): Khoi tao tu dau, Adam lr = 2e-3, 20 epochs, batch 64, chon checkpoint
    theo val_accuracy cao nhat.
  + Phase 2 (Refine): Ke thua Phase 1 best, Adam moi lr = 2e-4, 5 epochs, chon checkpoint
    theo val_accuracy cao nhat.
  + Phase 3 (Re-upload): Nap Phase 2 best, them re-upload scale, Adam tach 2 nhom LR:
    core_lr = 1e-4, readout_lr = 1e-3, ReduceLROnPlateau(factor=0.5, patience=3), toi da 60 epochs,
    early stopping patience 10 theo val_loss thap nhat.
- Ket qua Baseline dat duoc:
  + Checkpoint: fq_phase3_reupload_best.pt (nam tai full_quantum/full_quantum/ va trong full_quantum.zip).
  + Tai epoch 82: Validation loss = 0.074029, Validation accuracy = 97.71%.
  + Train accuracy cuoi cung (epoch 85): ~98.60% (loss ~0.0492).
