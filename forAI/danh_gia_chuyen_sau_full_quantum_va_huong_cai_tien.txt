3. DUONG DI DU LIEU VA MA HOA
================================================================================

3.1. Du lieu va split
- Script baseline run_convergence.py va script MLP+augmentation deu chia MNIST
  official train 60.000 mau thanh 10.000 validation dau tien va 50.000 train
  con lai theo torch.randperm voi split_seed=20260911.
- Validation khong duoc cap nhat gradient.
- Official MNIST test khong duoc dung trong baseline hien tai.
- Script MLP+augmentation tao hai dataset MNIST tach biet: train co RandomAffine,
  validation chi ToTensor. Day la cach dung de validation khong bi augmentation.

3.2. Baseline preprocessing
- MNIST vao model la grayscale 28x28, gia tri pixel [0,1].
- preprocess_full_quantum mac dinh mode="resize"; F.interpolate bilinear,
  align_corners=False dua anh ve 16x16.
- Ham downsample hien tai khong truyen antialias=True.
- Anh 16x16 duoc flatten row-major thanh 256 site.
- angle_ry_encode tao theta = pi * pixel va state
  cos(theta/2)|0> + sin(theta/2)|1>.
- Moi gia tri pixel duoc ma hoa thanh mot qubit state; khong co trainable image
  embedding truoc TN core.

3.3. He qua thuc te
- Resize 28x28 -> 16x16 lam giam so site va chi phi, nhung co the lam mat net
  nho, diem giao nhau, do day net hoac chi tiet phan biet mot so cap chu so.
- Augmentation trong MLP runner duoc ap dung tren anh 28x28 truoc khi model
  resize xuong 16x16. Vi vay augmentation va downsampling tac dong lien tiep
  len cung mot tin hieu; net mảnh sau xoay/dich co the bi thay doi khi resize.
- RandomAffine dang dat degrees=8, translate=0.05, fill=0; 5% tren chieu 28
  tuong duong toi da khoang 1.4 pixel theo moi truc. Interpolation khong duoc
  chi dinh trong code, nen phu thuoc default cua torchvision RandomAffine.
- Train accuracy trong runner MLP duoc tinh tren cac ban anh da augment ngau
  nhien; khong the so truc tiep voi train accuracy baseline tinh tren anh sach.

================================================================================
4. CAU TRUC TN CORE - DIEM MANH VA NUT THAT
================================================================================

4.1. So tang gop
QuantumTNCore kiem tra input_sites/final_qubits phai la luy thua cua 2.
Voi final_qubits=8:
- 16x16: 256/8=32=2^5, co 5 QuantumConvPoolBlock.
- 32x32: 1024/8=128=2^7, co 7 block.

4.2. Mot QuantumConvPoolBlock lam gi
- Chia cac site thanh cap lien tiep (0,1), (2,3), ... tren chuoi da flatten.
- Ghe density matrices 2x2 cua hai site thanh density matrix cap 4x4.
- Ap dung mot learned 2-qubit unitary conv, sau do mot learned 2-qubit unitary
  pool. Cung mot bo tham so gate duoc dung lai cho tat ca cap trong block.
- Trace qubit thu hai trong moi cap; chi giu density matrix 2x2 cua qubit song
  sot. Output co mot density matrix rieng cho moi site con lai.
- Block ke tiep ghep cac density matrix rieng nay nhu cac site doc lap.

4.3. Thong tin bi mat
- Sau khi trace qubit va chi truyen cac reduced one-site density matrices,
  tuong quan giua survivor cua cap nay voi survivor cua cap khac khong con trong
  trang thai dau vao block tiep theo.
- Vi vay day khong phai MPS co bond dimension duoc truyen qua cac tang. Cac
  ma tran 2x2 la marginal tung site; den cuoi moi ghep thanh product density.
- Day la nut that bieu dien co co so truc tiep trong code neu nhan dang chu so
  can tuong quan khong gian dai hon mot cap pixel.
- Tuy nhien, viec mat tuong quan khong chung minh accuracy dang bi gioi han
  boi no; can ablation/architecture moi de do anh huong.

4.4. Chuoi 1D va topology anh
- Anh 2D duoc flatten row-major. Ghe cap lien tiep tuong ung cac pixel ngang
  o tang dau, nhung cac tang tiep theo tiep tuc gop tren cay 1D.
- Model da co spatial_permutation ho tro "row", "column", "morton".
- FQ_ARCH baseline va FQ_ARCH cua script MLP khong truyen spatial_orders, nen
  mac dinh ba nhanh la ["row","row","row"].
- Ba nhanh co tham so core va readout rieng, nhung nhin cung thu tu pixel va
  duoc train bang cung loss. Chung co the tu hoc khac nhau, nhung khong co rang
  buoc nao dam bao ba nhanh phat trien chuyen mon/bo sung.
- [row,column,morton] se cho cac nhanh cung so site va cung so tham so nhung
  phan cap cac cap site khac nhau. Day la thay doi topology co san trong model,
  khong can them mot MLP lon.

4.5. Giai doan cuoi
- Sau cac tang pooling, 8 site duoc ghep thanh joint density matrix 256x256.
- StronglyEntanglingCore mac dinh co 4 layer. Moi layer co learned one-qubit
  rotations va cac learned two-qubit gates noi qubit theo range luan phien.
- Ba nhanh chay TN rieng tren cung encoded image, doc logits rieng va lay trung
  binh logits truoc cross-entropy.
- Reupload phase them 8 scale moi nhanh, tong 24 tham so. Cac scale bat dau
  bang 0; trong code reupload tinh goc trung binh input theo nhom site map vao
  tung qubit cuoi roi ap RY scale hoc duoc truoc merge stage.

4.6. Unitary gates
- LearnedUnitary2Q tao ma tran Hermitian tu raw_real/raw_imag va tinh
  matrix_exp(iH); real_only mode tao orthogonal matrix tu skew-symmetric.
- init_scale=0.01 lam raw parameters ban dau nho, nen unitary gan identity.
- Day la prior khoi tao, khong phai loi code. Khong nen thay init_scale dong
  thoi voi thay spatial order, readout va augmentation trong cung run.

================================================================================
5. READOUT: LINEAR BASELINE VA MLP VARIANT
================================================================================

5.1. Pauli full9 Linear baseline
- Readout doc 3*8=24 single-qubit expectations (X,Y,Z).
- Co 8 choose 2 = 28 cap qubit; moi cap doc 9 tuong quan
  XX,XY,XZ,YX,YY,YZ,ZX,ZY,ZZ, tong 252 feature.
- Tong 276 feature, dua vao Linear(276,10).
- Moi nhanh: 276*10 + 10 = 2.770 tham so.
- 3 nhanh: 8.310 tham so readout.
- Tong baseline duoc config/tai lieu ghi 13.110 tham so truoc reupload va
  13.134 sau khi them reupload.

5.2. Pauli MLP trong variant
- PauliMLPReadout thay lop Linear don bang:
  Linear(276,256) -> LayerNorm(256) -> GELU -> Dropout(0.1)
  -> Linear(256,10).
- Tinh tham so tren moi nhanh:
  + Linear 276->256: 276*256 + 256 = 70.912.
  + LayerNorm: 2*256 = 512.
  + Linear 256->10: 256*10 + 10 = 2.570.
  + Tong: 73.994 tham so/nhanh.
- Ba nhanh: 221.982 tham so readout, tang 213.672 so voi Linear full9.
- Tong model uoc tinh 226.782 tham so truoc reupload va 226.806 sau reupload.
- MLP khong lam tang so site hay depth TN, nhung tang kha nang phi tuyen tinh
  va tham so readout manh. Dropout chi active trong model.train().

5.3. Danh gia MLP
Uu diem:
- Co the hoc tuong tac phi tuyen giua cac Pauli features, trong khi linear head
  chi tao bien quyet dinh tuyen tinh tren 276 feature.
- LayerNorm/GELU/dropout tao mot head co regularization va phi tuyen tinh.

Han che:
- Features Pauli la cac expectation co mien gia tri co ban [-1,1]; MLP co the
  tang do uon bien quyet dinh, nhung khong tao them thong tin da mat trong TN
  pooling hay khi downsample anh.
- 256 hidden units tao head lon hon nhieu so voi phan TN; khong co ablation
  head 32/64/128 de biet quy mo can thiet.
- Baseline train-validation gap khoang 0.87 diem phan tram (train 98.58%,
  validation 97.71%), khong phai bang chung overfit nang. Do do, tang regularizer
  hoac tang head khong tu dong la uu tien cao hon cai thien bieu dien.
- MLP+augmentation runner doi hai nhom yeu to cung luc va train tu dau; neu ket
  qua tang/giam thi khong biet nguyen nhan la augmentation, MLP hay tuong tac.

Khuyen nghi neu can thu MLP:
- Thu hidden_dim=64 truoc 256. Tham so readout xap xi:
  276*64+64 + 2*64 + 64*10+10 = 18.506 moi nhanh,
  55.614 cho ba nhanh. Van lon hon Linear 8.310 nhung chi bang 1/4 head 256.
- Thu MLP-64 khong augmentation truoc. Sau do thu augmentation rieng tren
  readout baseline hoac tren cau hinh tot nhat.
- Khong khang dinh MLP-64 tot hon truoc khi co history/checkpoint.

7. HUONG CAI TIEN - XEP THEO UU TIEN VA GIA TRI THUC NGHIEM

UU TIEN 1: Ba spatial orders khac nhau [row,column,morton]
Muc tieu: cai thien inductive bias cua ba TN branches ma khong tang so nhanh
hay kich thuoc anh.

Can lam:
- Giu 16x16, 3 branches, full9 Linear, preprocess resize va lich train baseline.
- Chi doi spatial_orders tu mac dinh [row,row,row] thanh [row,column,morton].
- Code FullQuantumQCNN da ho tro permutation nay; runner hien tai can truyen
  spatial_orders vao FQ_ARCH/config de thí nghiem reproducible.
- Chay cung seed/split va so voi [row,row,row].

Vi sao uu tien:
- Code core pool cap theo thu tu site, nen permutation lam thay doi pixel nao
  duoc ghep va nhom som.
- Column co the thay doi huong gom, Morton co the dua cac diem trong patch 2D
  vao gan nhau som hon. Day la gia thuyet topology, khong bao dam accuracy.
- Chi phi va so tham so gan nhu khong doi; 3 branches van la 3 branches.

Rui ro:
- Morton mapping hien tai can duoc kiem tra truc quan/dinh nghia voi kich thuoc
  hinh; khong coi ket qua tot hon mot seed la ket luan.
- Neu warm-start checkpoint row-major, weights core da hoc theo row; doi order
  khi nap checkpoint la mismatch ve y nghia features. De ablation sach, train
  tu dau voi seed tuong ung, hoac ghi ro day la transfer/fine-tune khac.

UU TIEN 2: Anti-aliasing ablation cho 28x28 -> 16x16
Muc tieu: giam aliasing/mat net do downsampling, khong doi TN/readout.

Can lam:
- So F.interpolate bilinear hien tai voi bilinear antialias=True.

- Luu ra config de checkpoint co the tai lap.

- Input MNIST downsamplet hon 40% theo chieu, chi tiet net co the anh huong.
- Day la ablation re hon tang 32x32 va khong tang input sites.

Rui ro:
- Anti-alias lam net muot hon, co the xoa chi tiet huu ich. Chi la thí nghiem,
  khong tu dong tot hon.

UU TIEN 3: Warm-start augmentation nhe, khong doi readout
